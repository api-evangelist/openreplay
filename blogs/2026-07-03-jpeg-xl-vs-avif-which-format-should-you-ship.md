Recommends shipping AVIF for production web delivery in 2026 due to its ~93% global browser coverage, even though JPEG XL is technically stronger (faster encoding, better lossless, native progressive decoding) but remains limited mostly to Safari. Provides compression, browser support, and encoding cost comparisons.
