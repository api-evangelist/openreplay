JSON-LD embeds Schema.org vocabulary as structured, machine-readable metadata in HTML, enabling search engines and AI crawlers to identify entities like articles, organizations, products, and breadcrumbs without inferring them from text. Correct JSON-LD makes pages eligible for specific Google Search features and helps populate the Knowledge Graph, which is increasingly important as AI systems use structured data to understand web content.
